Modifications by ZTARK (windowfilmresources-prog, 2026-10):
- De-identified: nose and tail emblems, rear lettering and wheel-cap logos
  removed.
- Interior: the source file's seats, steering wheel and dashboard tub were
  removed (their object names mark them as third-party parts, not the
  author's own work) and replaced with new geometry made by ZTARK - seats,
  dashboard, centre console, door cards, steering wheel, load bay. An opaque
  sheet under the back glass was removed so the window is see-through.
- Geometry reduced from about 2.6M to 0.53M triangles (one subdivision level
  removed where the mesh allowed, tyres and calipers decimated); the missing
  fourth brake caliper and the two missing wheel centre caps were completed
  from the car's own parts.
- Materials rebuilt (body paint -> Body_Paint, glass split into window panes
  and lamp lenses, rims, lamps). Draco-compressed, no textures.
